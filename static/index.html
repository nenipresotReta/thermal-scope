Probe response

Probe geometry: Ø3 mm stainless shell, 0.2 mm wall (assumed 304). Shell-only estimate: τ ≈ 737 / h seconds, with h in W/(m²·K). Internal filling and element contact remain unknown. The effective thermal model predicts probe readings; internal probe/filter lag is not separately identified.

This measures thermal response, not temperature accuracy. WATER and AIR are separate profiles; neither automatically describes a coffee bed.

  1. Let the probe settle at room temperature.
  2. Select WATER, press START STEP, then immerse the sensing tip in well-mixed hot water. Keep the connector dry.
  3. Wait until nearly stable, then FINISH & FIT.
  4. For the reverse step, select AIR, start, then move the probe back to the same room environment. Air response may take several minutes.
